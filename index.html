About The Band

We are a four piece group that make songs based on over 100 years of combined experience in original music making.

We are genre blending, and our bios show an eclectic blend of bands as our influences.

We hope to portray warmth, clarity, and dynamic range. We believe music is one way people communicate mortally and spiritually.  We provide unique and thought provoking messages, so our legacy will be reflected in the continuation of musical art in those we inspire. 

Our songs are distinct: while each song stands on its own, we want the listener to hear a cohesive final outcome.  We present a detailed, clean, pristine mix that is balanced, polished, versatile.

Our view is that our audience experiences a harmonic and rich result. Our genre is diverse, but generally falls within the revival of pure instruments and hooks; with guitars, strong vocals behind poetic lyrics, and keyboards being prominent. We use electronic drums- though we hear the heart of a natural drum as a connection to the first of humankind who created music.

We do not in any way use AI to generate our songs. We do use AI to generate videos. We do so for humor and listener amusement. Using its own tools to promote human consciousness of AI’s danger - irony!

If you enjoy us, tell us why; and share us, so our “us” grows to all of us.
